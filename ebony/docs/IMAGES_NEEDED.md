# EBONY Photography Guide: Kitchen Style Shots

This document lists the photography requirements for the 12 kitchen styles in EBONY's modular collection. While photorealistic AI visual representations have been generated and deployed in `public/img/`, real photographs of completed customer installations in Raipur, Bilaspur, and Chhattisgarh can be shot to these exact specifications.

---

## Technical Camera & Lighting Standards
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 4:3 landscape (min 2400 × 1600 px).
- **Lighting:** Warm interior architectural lighting (3000K) combined with natural daylight. Turn under-cabinet LED profile lights on.
- **Styling:** Neatly styled with fresh herbs, ceramic stoneware, minimalist glassware, or subtle cookware. Avoid brand logos on appliances.
- **Perspective:** One-point or two-point perspective eye-level shot (~1.2m to 1.4m height). Keep vertical cabinet lines straight.

## File Upload Instructions
When real site photographs are captured:
1. Export JPGs at 85% quality, sRGB color profile, max 400KB per image.
2. Save into `public/img/` matching the filenames above.
3. The website components automatically pick up the new photography without code changes!
