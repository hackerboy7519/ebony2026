# EBONY — Inspiration & Adaptation Log (Phase 0 Discovery)

Analysis of 6 designated reference sources and the 10 core architectural patterns adapted into the EBONY redesign:

---

## 1. Reference Sources Reviewed
1. **ThreeUI (threeui.com/three-js)**:
   - *Adapted Idea 1: Halcyon Card* — Gold foil hairlines and specular accents on an engraved, tactile light surface. Applied to hero floating status badges and collection bezel cards.
   - *Adapted Idea 2: Wood Icons & Precision Latches* — Smooth mechanical drawer and hinge movement semantics adapted into interactive section states.
   - *Adapted Idea 3: Isometric Motion Grid* — Subtle pale coordinate grid lines on the Atelier Daylight canvas (0.5px hairlines at 20% opacity) anchoring 3D objects and typography.
2. **MotionSites (motionsites.ai)**:
   - *Adapted Idea 4: Asymmetric Architectural Layouts* — High-contrast editorial typography paired with uncentered, dramatic negative space rather than boring centered stacks.
   - *Adapted Idea 5: Light Sweep & Foil Shimmer* — CSS mask and linear-gradient background clip simulating sunlight moving across brushed metallic and lacquered panels.
3. **React Bits (reactbits.dev)**:
   - *Adapted Idea 6: SplitText & BlurText Reveals* — Word-by-word headline reveals with subtle translateY and blur reduction on scroll entry.
   - *Adapted Idea 7: CountUp with Scrubbed Trigger* — Numerical statistics count smoothly from 0 to 15+, 85%+, 300+, 10+ when entering the viewport.
   - *Adapted Idea 8: TiltedCard / Spotlight Surface* — Interactive cards that respond gracefully to mouse hover with micro-elevation and radial sheen.
4. **GSAP Showcase (gsap.com/showcase)**:
   - *Adapted Idea 9: Scrubbed Pin Sequence* — Coordinated timeline pinning for the "3 Days to a New Home" multi-stage transformation and the vertical integration steps.
5. **Modern Web Guidance & Architectural Cleanliness**:
   - *Adapted Idea 10: Anti-Slop Discipline* — Eliminating generic blue/purple neon blobs, heavy videos, or fake AI clichés. Celebrating genuine German engineering, modular craftsmanship, and quiet luxury.
