## 2. Brand Positioning & Aesthetic
- **Concept**: "Atelier Daylight" — quiet luxury, architectural clarity, and warmth.
- **Theme**: Light theme. Pearl-white (#FBFBF9) surfaces, mist (#F0EFEA) alternations, ebony-black typography (#0B0B0C), with champagne and warm gold foil accents (#B8923A, #7C5F16).
- **Gold Usage Rule**: Gold foil covers ≤8% of any viewport as an accent of refinement (hairlines, handles, typography highlights, numerals), never garish flood fills.
- **Audience**: Affluent Indian homeowners, architects, interior designers, builders, franchise partners, and dealers.
