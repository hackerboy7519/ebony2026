# EBONY — Section Storyboard (Phase 1 Art Direction)

**Master Narrative: "From a thought to a home."**
A seamless journey through precision, craftsmanship, materials, collections, and execution.

---

### Section 1: Hero — The Opening Door
- **Purpose**: Immediate emotional impact of quiet luxury. Introduce the founding vision and German engineering credentials.
- **Visual & Layout**: Asymmetric layout. Left: Staggered typography reveals with gold foil accents and floating stats badge. Right: Hero culinary visual with gold hairline border and ambient pulse badge.
- **Motion**: GSAP entrance timeline with clip-path wipe, word blur reduction, and subtle floating parallax.
- **Fallback**: Static high-res poster image for low-power or reduced-motion devices.

---

### Section 2: Philosophy — Crafting Spaces That Outlive Their Moment
- **Purpose**: Deepen the brand ethos of EBONY Modular Kulture (established 2009).
- **Visual & Layout**: Split layout. Left: Large editorial italic serif headline with gold gradient hairline. Right: Three numbered pillar cards (01 Visionary Design, 02 German Engineering, 03 Enduring Craft).
- **Motion**: ScrollTrigger 3D word unrolling on heading, staggered card entrance with gold hover accentuation.

---

### Section 3: Interactive Finish Studio — 500+ Finishes
- **Purpose**: Tangible demonstration of variety and German surface technology.
- **Visual & Layout**: Interactive material visualizer showcasing Super Matt, Crystal Glass, Acrylic Gloss, Ebony Grain, Champagne Metallic, and Fluted Walnut. Live selector ring with active gold ring, material specs, reflection index, and "Your Selection" pre-fill chip.
- **Motion**: Instant PBR surface preview swap, smooth micro-animation on swatch selection, responsive to touch and keyboard navigation.

---

### Section 4: Collections — Three Worlds of Mastery
- **Purpose**: Showcase the three core product categories with real counts.
- **Visual & Layout**: 3-column bezel cards:
  - 01: Modular Kitchen ("Culinary Spaces" · 300+ designs)
  - 02: Wardrobe ("Personal Sanctuaries" · 100+ configurations)
  - 03: Bed Set ("Sleep in Luxury" · 50+ collections)
- **Motion**: Staggered scroll entrance, card image zoom on hover (scale 1.05), gold arrow slide.

---

### Section 5: 3 Days to a New Home — Rapid Installation Timeline
- **Purpose**: Overcome the homeowner fear of chaotic, unending carpenter renovations.
- **Visual & Layout**: Interactive timeline scrubber / multi-stage display comparing:
  - Day 1: Carcass & Modular Framework
  - Day 2: Doors, Hardware & German Concealed Hinges
  - Day 3: Finished, Styled Luxury Living
- **Motion**: GSAP timeline wipe transition with day counter and stage completion indicators.

---

### Section 6: Craft & Precision Engineering
- **Purpose**: Detail the vertical integration and German manufacturing machinery.
- **Visual & Layout**: Split grid pairing high-resolution engineering workshop photography with four sequenced milestones (Design, Manufacturing, Engineering, Installation).
- **Motion**: Clip-path reveal on workshop image, gold line drawing on step hover.

---

### Section 7: By the Numbers — Proven Track Record
- **Purpose**: High-credibility validation for affluent homeowners and prospective franchise partners.
- **Visual & Layout**: Ebony-dark contrast band with four gold foil numerals: 15+ Years, 85%+ Retention, 300+ Dealers, 10+ Franchises.
- **Motion**: GSAP ScrollTrigger CountUp animating values when 50% visible, subtle gold foil shimmer.

---

### Section 8: Pan-India Network & Interactive City Presence
- **Purpose**: Reassure clients of regional availability and invite dealer/franchise partnerships.
- **Visual & Layout**: Stylized India network map with glowing nodes (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, Ahmedabad, Raipur), active city detail cards, and direct dealer inquiry action.
- **Motion**: Pulsing gold node rings, interactive click-to-focus on cities.

---

### Section 9: Testimonials — Verified Client Voices
- **Purpose**: Social proof directly using verbatim words from live site clients (Manoj Mandre, Mr. Kanyalal, C.L. Sandesh, Ramesh Goradia, Rupali Rach, Anuska Hormaz).
- **Visual & Layout**: Elegant review card slider with 5 gold stars, quotation marks, homeowner badges, and location tags.
- **Motion**: Smooth draggable/scrollable carousel with gold card borders on focus.

---

### Section 10: Consultation CTA & Architectural Footer
- **Purpose**: Lead capture (book consultation, dealer enquiry) with zero friction.
- **Visual & Layout**:
  - Left: Interactive consultation form with interest tags, city picker, contact fields, and invisible honeypot spam protection.
  - Right: Quick direct contact cards for phone click-to-call, WhatsApp click-to-chat, email, and Raipur studio.
  - Footer: 4-column ebony footer with brand mark, sitemap links, and copyright 2026.
